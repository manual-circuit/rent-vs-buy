# Vermont State Income Tax Withholding

Reference Number: NFC-26-1780320782
Published: June 9, 2026
Effective: Pay Period 09, 2026

## Summary

The State of Vermont has the following changes:

- The annual amount per exemption for the State of Vermont is increasing from $5,300 to $5,400.
- The Single and Married tax tables have been updated as described in the tables below.

No action on the part of the employee or the personnel office is necessary.

### Withholding Formula (Effective Pay Period 09, 2026)

1. Subtract the nontaxable biweekly Thrift Savings Plan contribution from the gross biweekly wages.

2. Subtract the nontaxable biweekly Federal Employees Health Benefits Plan payment(s) (includes dental and vision insurance program and Flexible Spending Account — health care and dependent care deductions) from the amount computed in step 1.

3. Add the taxable biweekly fringe benefits (e.g., taxable life insurance) to the amount computed in step 2 to obtain the adjusted gross biweekly wages.

4. Multiply the adjusted gross biweekly wages by the number of pay dates in the tax year to obtain the gross annual wages.

5. Determine the exemption allowance by applying the following guideline and subtract this amount from the annual wages to compute taxable income:

   Multiply the number of Allowances by $5,400.

6. Apply the taxable income calculated in step 5 to the following tables to determine the annual Vermont tax withholding:

#### Single or Head of Household Tax Withholding Table

| If the Amount of Taxable Income Is: | The Amount of Tax Withholding Should Be: |
|---|---|
| Over $0 but not over $3,925 | $0.00 |
| Over $3,925 but not over $54,675 | $0.00 plus 3.35% over $3,925 |
| Over $54,675 but not over $126,775 | $1,700.13 plus 6.60% of excess over $54,675 |
| Over $126,775 but not over $260,225 | $6,458.73 plus 7.60% of excess over $126,775 |
| Over $260,225 | $16,600.93 plus 8.75% of excess over<br>$260,225 |

Married Tax Withholding Table

| If the Amount of Taxable Income Is: | The Amount of Tax Withholding Should Be: |
|---|---|
| Over $0 but not over $11,775 | $0.00 |
| Over $11,775 but not over $96,475 | $0.00 plus 3.35% over $11,775 |
| Over $96,475 but not over $216,525 | $2,837.45 plus 6.60% of excess over $96,475 |
| Over $216,525 but not over $323,825 | $10,760.75 plus 7.60% of excess over $216,525 |
| Over $323,825 | $18,915.55 plus 8.75% of excess over $323,825 |

7. Divide the annual tax withholding calculated in step 6 by the number of pay dates in the tax year to obtain the biweekly Vermont tax withholding. If Federal allowances were used and there are additional Federal withholdings, proceed to step 8.
8. If additional Federal tax was withheld, multiply the additional amount by 30 percent and add that to the result of step 7 to obtain the biweekly Vermont tax withholding.
