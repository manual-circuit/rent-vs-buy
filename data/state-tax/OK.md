# Oklahoma State Income Tax Withholding

Reference Number: NFC-26-1773324603
Published: March 13, 2026
Effective: Pay Period 03, 2026

## Summary

The Single and Married tax tables for the State of Oklahoma have changed. No action on the part of the employee or the personnel office is necessary.

## Tax Formula

| State Abbreviation: | OK |
|---|---|
| State Tax Withholding State Code: | 40 |
| Acceptable Exemption Form: | OK W-4 |
| Basis for Withholding: | State or Federal Exemptions |
| Acceptable Exemption Data: | S/M/H, Number of Exemptions |
| TSP Deferred: | Yes |
| Special Coding: | None |
| Additional Information: | None |

### Withholding Formula (Effective Pay Period 03, 2026)

1. Subtract the nontaxable biweekly Thrift Savings Plan (TSP) contribution from the gross biweekly wages.
2. Subtract the nontaxable biweekly Federal Health Benefits Plan payment(s) (includes dental and vision insurance program and Flexible Spending Account - health care and dependent care deductions) from the amount computed in step 1.
3. Add the taxable biweekly fringe benefits (e.g., taxable life insurance) to the amount computed in step 2 to obtain the adjusted gross biweekly wages.
4. Multiply the adjusted gross biweekly wages by the number of pay dates in the tax year to obtain the gross annual wages.
5. Determine the exemption allowance by applying the following guideline and subtracting this amount from the results in step 4 to compute the taxable income:

Multiply the number of exemptions by $1,000

6. Apply the taxable income computed in step 5 to the following table(s) to determine the annual Oklahoma tax withholding:

#### Single or Head of Household Tax Withholding Table

|  |  |
|---|---|
| If the Amount of Taxable Income Is: | The Amount of Tax Withholding Should Be: |
| Over $0 but not over $10,100 | $0.00 |
| Over $10,100 but not over $11,250 | $0.00 plus 2.50% of excess over $10,100 |
| Over $11,250 but not over $13,550 | $28.75 plus 3.50% of excess over $11,250 |
| Over $13,550 | $109.25 plus 4.50% of excess over $13,550 |

#### Married Tax Withholding Table

| If the Amount of Taxable Income Is: | The Amount of Tax Withholding Should Be: |
|---|---|
| Over $0 but not over $20,200 | $0.00 |
| Over $20,200 but not over $22,500 | $0.00 plus 2.50% of excess over $20,200 |
| Over $22,500 but not over $27,100 | $57.50 plus 3.50% of excess over $22,500 |
| Over $27,100 | $218.50 plus 4.50% of excess over $27,100 |

7. Divide the annual Oklahoma tax withholding calculated in step 6 by the number of pay dates in the tax year and round to the nearest dollar to obtain the biweekly Oklahoma tax withholding.
8. Add the additional amount or percentage elected by the employee to the pay period tax calculated in step 7 and round to the nearest dollar to determine the amount of tax to be withheld for the pay period.
