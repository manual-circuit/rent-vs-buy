# Arkansas State Income Tax Withholding

Reference Number: NFC-26-1781190112
Published: August 24, 2026
Effective: Pay Period 15, 2026

## Summary

The State of Arkansas has the following changes:

- The standard tax table is changing.
- The tax credit formula for married employees claiming 2 or more exemptions who qualify for the low-income tax tables has been modified.

No action on the part of the employee or the personnel office is necessary.

### Withholding Formula (Effective Pay Period 15, 2026)

1. Subtract the nontaxable biweekly Thrift Savings Plan contribution from the gross biweekly wages.
2. Subtract the nontaxable biweekly Federal Employees Health Benefits Plan payment(s) (includes dental and vision insurance program and Flexible Spending Account — health care and dependent care deductions) from the amount computed in step 1.
3. Add the taxable biweekly fringe benefits (e.g., taxable life insurance) to the amount computed in step 2 to obtain the adjusted gross biweekly wages.
4. Multiply the adjusted gross biweekly wages by the number of pay dates in the tax year to obtain the gross annual wages.
5. Subtract the Standard Deduction of $2,470 from annualized gross pay in step 4 to determine annual net taxable income.
6. If the annual net taxable income is less than $100,001, truncate (shorten) the annual net taxable income to the nearest $100, and then add $50. If the annual net taxable income is $100,001 or more, there is no adjustment to the annual net taxable income amount.

Example: If the annual net taxable income is $23,324, truncate to $23,300, and add $50. The amount to be used in calculating the annual gross tax amount is $23,350.

8. If the employee has elected to use the low income tax tables, calculate the low income tax credit based on filing status using the formula below. If the result is less than zero, the low income tax credit is zero. Reduce the annual gross tax calculated in the previous step by the low income tax credit. Use the employee's annual wages, calculated in step 4, in the tax credit formula.

|  |  |
|---|---|
| Filing Status | Tax Credit Formula |
| Single | (1 - (Annual Wages - $14,644*) / ($17,500 - $14,644)) x $111.80 |
| Married (<= 1 exemption) | (1 - (Annual Wages - $24,696*) / ($29,000 - $24,696)) x $391.56 |
| Married (>=2 exemptions) | (1 - (Annual Wages - $29,723*) / ($36,100 - $29,723)) x $531.96 |
| Head of Household (<=1 exemption) | (1 - (Annual Wages - $20,821*) / ($25,300 - $20,821)) x $268.84 |
| Head of Household (>=2 exemptions) | (1 - (Annual Wages - $24,819*) / ($29,000 - $24,819)) x $378.04 |

* If less than zero, the tax credit is equal to the standard tax and the pay period tax is zero.

9. Multiply the total number of withholding exemptions by $29 to determine the personal tax credit.
10. Subtract the personal tax credit from the annual gross tax in step 7 to determine the annual net tax. The result cannot be less than zero.
11. Divide the Arkansas annual net tax withholding determined by the number of pay dates in the tax year to obtain the biweekly Arkansas income tax withholding.
