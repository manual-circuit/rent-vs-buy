# Kentucky State Income Tax Withholding

Reference Number: NFC-26-1770145550
Published: March 3, 2026
Effective: Pay Period 02, 2026

## Summary

The income tax withholding formula for the State of Kentucky includes the following changes:

- The annual standard deduction has changed from $3,270 to $3,360.
- The tax rate has changed from 4.0% to 3.5%.

No action on the part of the employee or the personnel office is necessary.

### Withholding Formula (Effective Pay Period 02, 2026)

1. Subtract the nontaxable biweekly Thrift Savings Plan contribution from the gross biweekly wages.
2. Subtract the nontaxable biweekly Federal Health Benefits Plan payment(s) (includes dental and vision insurance program and flexible spending account-health care and dependent care deductions) from the amount computed in step 1.
3. Add the taxable biweekly fringe benefits (e.g., taxable life insurance) to the amount computed in step 2 to obtain the adjusted gross biweekly wages.
4. Multiply the adjusted gross biweekly wages by the number of pay dates in the tax year to obtain the gross annual wages.
5. Subtract the standard deduction of $3,360 from the result of step 4 to compute the taxable income.
6. Apply the flat tax rate of 3.5 percent to the annual taxable wages in step 5 to determine the annual tax amount.
7. Divide the annual Kentucky tax withholding calculated in step 6 by the number of pay dates in the tax year to obtain the biweekly Kentucky tax withholding.
