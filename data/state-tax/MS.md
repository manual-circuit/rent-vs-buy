# Mississippi State Income Tax Withholding

Reference Number: NFC-26-1768327516
Published: June 9, 2026
Effective: Pay Period 09, 2026

## Summary

The income tax table for the State of Mississippi has changed.

No action on the part of the employee or the personnel office is necessary.

## Tax Formula

|  |  |
|---|---|
| State Abbreviation: | MS |
| State Tax Withholding State Code: | 28 |
| Acceptable Exemption Form: | 89-350-25-8-1-000 (Rev. 10/25) |
| Basis for Withholding: | Three-digit number from special coding & Federal marital status |
| Acceptable Exemption Data: | Three digits from special coding |
| TSP Deferred: | Yes |
| Special Coding: | Determine the Exemption Data field as follows:<br>Divide the dollar amount in item 6 of the State certificate by 100. Multiply the result by 2. (Examples are 120 for $6,000, 190 for $9,500, and 240 for $12,000.) |
| Additional Information: | In the event that the employee does not file a State Withholding Allowance Certificate, Single and zero personal exemptions (S00) will be used as the basis for withholding. |

### Withholding Formula (Effective Pay Period 09, 2026)

1. Subtract the nontaxable biweekly Thrift Savings Plan contribution from the gross biweekly wages.
2. Subtract the nontaxable biweekly Federal Employees Health Benefits Plan payment(s) (includes dental and vision insurance program and Flexible Spending Account — health care and dependent care deductions) from the amount computed in step 1.
3. Add the taxable biweekly fringe benefits (e.g., taxable life insurance) to the amount computed in step 2 to obtain the adjusted gross biweekly wages.
4. Multiply the adjusted gross biweekly wages by the number of pay dates in the tax year to obtain the gross annualized wages.
5. Determine the standard deduction by applying the following guideline and subtract this amount from the gross annualized wages to compute the taxable income:

| If the Employee Is: | The Standard Deduction Is: |
|---|---|
| Without a W-4 on file | $2,300 |
| Single | $2,300 |
| Married - Both Spouses Working | $2,300 |
| Head of Household | $3,400 |
| Married | $4,600 |

6. Subtract the following amounts from annualized taxable wages.

   a. Subtract the standard personal exemption amount based on W-4 marital status:

      | If the Employee Is: | The Standard Personal Exemption Is: |
      |---|---|
      | Without a W-4 on file | $0 |
      | Single | $6,000 |
      | Head of Household | $9,500 |
      | Married - Both Spouses Working | $12,000 |
      | Married | $12,000 |

   b. Multiply exemptions withheld on W-4 by $1,500 each and subtract from taxable income.

Note: The exemption allowance is the Total Amount of Exemption Claimed entered in Item 6 on the Mississippi Employee's Withholding Exemption Certificate, Form 89-350-25-8-1-000 (Rev. 10/25).

7. Apply the taxable income in step 6 to the following table to determine the annualized Mississippi tax withholding:

#### Tax Withholding Table

| If the Amount of Taxable Income Is: | The Amount of Tax Withholding Should Be: |
|---|---|
| Over $0 but not over $10,000 | $0 |
| Over $10,000 | $0 plus 4.0% of excess over $10,000 |

8. Divide the annualized Mississippi tax withholding calculated in step 7 by the number of pay dates in the tax year and round to the nearest dollar to obtain the biweekly Mississippi tax withholding.
9. Add the additional amount or percentage elected by the employee to the pay period tax calculated in step 8 and round to the nearest dollar to determine the amount of tax to be withheld for the pay period.
