# Utah State Income Tax Withholding

Reference Number: NFC-26-1782763921
Published: July 6, 2026
Effective: Pay Period 12, 2026

## Summary

The income tax withholding formula for the State of Utah includes the following changes:

- The income tax rate has decreased from 4.50 percent to 4.45 percent.
- The base allowance for Single filers has increased from $450 to $485.
- The base allowance for Married filers has increased from $900 to $970.
- The wages exempt from tax credit reduction for Single filers has increased from $9,107 to $9,348.
- The wages exempt from tax credit reduction for Married filers has increased from $18,213 to $18,696.

No action on the part of the employee or the personnel office is necessary.

### Withholding Formula (Effective Pay Period 12, 2026)

1. Subtract the nontaxable biweekly Thrift Savings Plan contribution from the gross biweekly wages.

2. Subtract the nontaxable biweekly Federal Employees Health Benefits Plan payment(s) (includes dental and vision insurance program, and Flexible Spending Account — health care and dependent care deductions) from the adjusted gross biweekly wages.

3. Add taxable biweekly fringe benefits (e.g., taxable life insurance) to the amount computed in step 2 to obtain the adjusted gross biweekly wages.

4. Multiply the adjusted gross biweekly wages by the number of pay dates in the tax year to obtain the gross annual wages.

5. Multiply the annual taxable wages by 4.45 percent to determine the annual gross tax amount.

6. Calculate the annual withholding allowance. If the employee has not filed a W-4, then the annual withholding allowance is zero.

   a. Subtract the following amount from the employee's annual taxable wages, but not less than zero:

      | Marital Status: | Amount: |
      |---|---|
      | Single | $9,348 |
      | Married | $18,696 |

   b. Multiply the amount calculated in step 6a by 1.3 percent to determine the annual withholding allowance reduction.

   c. Subtract the annual withholding allowance reduction from the following Base Allowance, but not less than zero; the result is the annual withholding allowance.

      | Marital Status: | Base Allowance: |
      |---|---|
      | Single | $485 |
      | Married | $970 |

7. Subtract the annual net withholding allowance in step 6c from the annual gross tax amount to determine the employee's annual net tax.

8. Divide the annual tax withholding calculated above by the number of pay dates in the tax year to obtain the biweekly tax withholding.
