# Idaho State Income Tax Withholding

Reference Number: NFC-26-1786997961
Published: September 23, 2026
Effective: Pay Period 17, 2026

## Summary

The income tax withholding formula for the State of Idaho has the following changes:

- The annual deduction per exemption has decreased from $3,868 to $0 and is no longer used to reduce taxable wages.
- The Single and Married tax tables have increased.

No action on the part of the employee or the personnel office is necessary.

Withholding Formula (Effective Pay Period 17, 2026)

1. Subtract the nontaxable biweekly Thrift Savings Plan contribution from the gross biweekly wages.
2. Subtract the nontaxable biweekly Federal Employees Health Benefits Plan payment(s) (includes dental and vision insurance program and Flexible Spending Account — health care and dependent care deductions) from the amount computed in step 1.
3. Add the taxable biweekly fringe benefits (e.g., taxable life insurance) to the amount computed in step 2 to obtain the adjusted gross biweekly wages.
4. Multiply the adjusted gross biweekly wages by the number of pay dates in the tax year to obtain the gross annualized wages.

Note: The annual deduction per exemption is no longer used to reduce taxable wages.

5. Apply the taxable income computed in step 4 to the following table to determine the annual tax withholding:

   #### Single - Tax Withholding Table

   | If the Amount of Taxable Income Is: | The Amount of Tax Withholding Should Be: |
   |---|---|
   | Over $0 but not over $16,100 | $0.00 |
   | Over $16,100 | 5.3% of excess over $16,100 |

   #### Married - Tax Withholding Table

   | If the Amount of Taxable Income Is: | The Amount of Tax Withholding Should Be: |
   |---|---|
   | Over $0 but not over $32,200 | $0.00 |
   | Over $32,200 | 5.3% of excess over $32,200 |

6. Divide the annual tax withholding calculated above by the number of pay dates in the tax year and round to the nearest dollar to obtain the biweekly tax withholding.

7. Add the additional amount or percentage elected by the employee to the pay period tax calculated in the above step and round to the nearest dollar to determine the amount of tax to be withheld.
