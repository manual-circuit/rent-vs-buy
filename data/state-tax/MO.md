# Missouri State Income Tax Withholding

Reference Number: NFC-26-1773783048
Published: May 14, 2026
Effective: Pay Period 08, 2026

## Summary

The income tax withholding formula for the State of Missouri has changed effective January 1, 2026, and includes the following changes:

- The standard deduction amount for employees claiming Single, Married Filing Separate, and Married and Spouse Works, has changed from $15,000 to $16,100.
- The standard deduction amount for employees claiming Married and Spouse Does Not Work has changed from $30,000 to $32,200.
- The standard deduction amount for employees claiming Head of Household has changed from $22,500 to $24,150.
- The income tax table has changed.

No action is required on the part of the employee or the personnel office.

### Withholding Formula (Effective Pay Period 08, 2026)

1. Subtract the nontaxable biweekly Thrift Savings Plan contribution from the gross biweekly wages.
2. Subtract the nontaxable biweekly Federal Employees Health Benefits Plan payment(s) (includes dental and vision insurance program and Flexible Spending Account — health care and dependent care deductions) from the amount computed in step 1.
3. Add the taxable biweekly fringe benefits (e.g., taxable life insurance) to the amount computed in step 2 to obtain the adjusted gross biweekly wages.
4. Multiply the adjusted gross biweekly wages by the number of pay dates in the tax year to obtain the annual wages.
5. Determine the standard deduction by applying the following guideline and subtract this amount from the annual wages in step 4:

| If the Employee Is: | The Standard Deduction Is: |
|---|---|
| Single | $16,100 |
| Married Filing Separate | $16,100 |
| Married and Spouse Works | $16,100 |
| Married and Spouse Does Not Work | $32,200 |
| Head of Household | $24,150 |

6. Apply the taxable income computed in step 5 to the following table to obtain the annual Missouri tax withholding:

#### Tax Withholding Table

| If the Amount of Taxable Income Is: | The Amount of Tax Withholding Should Be: |
|---|---|
| Over $0 but not over $1,348 | 0.0% |
| Over $1,348 but not over $2,696 | $0.00 plus 2.0% of excess over $1,348 |
| Over $2,696 but not over $4,044 | $27.00 plus 2.5% of excess over $2,696 |
| Over $4,044 but not over $5,392 | $61.00 plus 3.0% of excess over $4,044 |
| Over $5,392 but not over $6,740 | $101.00 plus 3.5% of excess over $5,392 |
| Over $6,740 but not over $8,088 | $148.00 plus 4.0% of excess over $6,740 |
| Over $8,088 but not over $9,436 | $202.00 plus 4.5% of excess over $8,088 |
| Over $9,436 | $263.00 plus 4.70% of excess over $9,436 |

7. Divide the annual Missouri tax withholding calculated in step 6 by the number of pay dates in the tax year and round to the nearest dollar to obtain the biweekly Missouri tax withholding.
8. Add the additional amount or percentage elected by the employee to the pay period tax calculated in step 7 and round to the nearest dollar to determine the amount of tax to be withheld for the pay period.
