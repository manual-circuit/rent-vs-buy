# Virginia State Income Tax Withholding

Reference Number: NFC-25-1750694986
Published: July 31, 2025
Effective: Pay Period 14, 2025

## Summary

The annual standard deduction amount has changed from $8,500 to $8,750 for the State of Virginia.

No action on the part of the employee or the personnel office is necessary.

### Withholding Formula (Effective Pay Period 14, 2025)

1. Subtract the biweekly Thrift Savings Plan (TSP) contribution from the gross biweekly wages.

2. Subtract the nontaxable biweekly Federal Health Benefits Plan payment(s) (includes dental and vision insurance programs, and Flexible Spending Account - health care and dependent care deductions) from the amount computed in step 1.

3. Add taxable biweekly fringe benefits (e.g., taxable life insurance) to the amount computed in step 2 to obtain the adjusted gross biweekly wages.

4. Multiply the adjusted gross biweekly wages by the number of pay dates in the tax year to obtain the gross annual wages.

5. Determine the exemption allowance by applying the following guideline and subtract this amount from the result of step 4 to determine annual taxable income:

   a. Subtract $8,750 from the annual gross wages.
   b. Multiply the number of personal and dependent exemptions claimed, not including the number of exemptions claimed for age and blindness, by $930.
   c. Multiply the number of exemptions claimed for age and blindness by $800.

6. Apply the taxable income computed in step 5 to the following table to determine the Virginia tax withholding:

Tax Withholding Tables

| If the Amount of Taxable Income Is: | The Amount of Virginia Tax Withholding Should Be: |
|---|---|
| Over $0 but not over $3,000 | 2.0% |
| Over $3,000 but not over $5,000 | $60 plus 3.0% of excess over $3,000 |
| Over $5,000 but not over $17,000 | $120 plus 5.0% of excess over $5,000 |
| Over $17,000 | $720 plus 5.75% of excess over $17,000 |

7. Divide the annual Virginia tax withholding calculated in step 6 by the number of pay dates in the tax year to obtain the biweekly Virginia tax withholding.
